A few things to know.

Does this recreate my mind?

No. A MINDPRINT is an editable guide to observed preferences and decision patterns. It helps an AI adapt its approach; it cannot replicate you or act with your authority.

How do I make one?

Copy the creation prompt and send it to your AI. It points to the open-source creation skill. Your agent can use context you choose to share or interview you. This website doesn’t generate your profile.

What happens when I share it?

The share link contains a snapshot of your reviewed profile. Anyone with the link can read it. It is not encrypted, cannot be revoked, and does not update when you edit your file. You can also share the Markdown file directly.

Is MINDPRINT an official industry standard?

It is an independent, open-source proposal, currently draft v0.1. It is not affiliated with MCP, Agent Skills, Apple, or any AI provider. You can read the specification and contribute on GitHub.
