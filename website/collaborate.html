Context, with consent.

A shared profile helps AI understand someone’s stated preferences. It cannot speak for them, approve a decision, or grant access to their work.

Keep each person’s perspective distinct. Ask for corrections. Share only reviewed profiles you have permission to use. Share links are unencrypted snapshots, readable by anyone with the link, and cannot be revoked.
