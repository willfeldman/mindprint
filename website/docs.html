About MINDPRINT

Make your perspective portable

MINDPRINT is a proposed open standard for describing how a person approaches work. Conditional habits, decision rules, communication preferences, and exceptions — in a file any text-capable AI can read.

How you prefer to approach it.

An independent, community-editable proposal. No affiliation with MCP or Agent Skills. No claims of universal native support.

Useful patterns. Fewer private details.

Distill the working habit. Leave out the names, secrets, and raw conversations. Review before sharing: preferences are still personal information.

A working model. Always editable.

Your MINDPRINT helps an AI adapt to you. It doesn’t replicate your mind, speak on your behalf, or replace your judgment.

Plain Markdown. No new silo.

Attach it, paste it, or use a skill adapter. Keep the file. Change the agent. Read the draft specification ›.

Get started

Create your MINDPRINT

Create your MINDPRINT with AI, then bring the finished file back here.

Create it with your AI.

Your MINDPRINT should come from a conversation with an AI that can explore your patterns, ask follow-up questions, and help you refine the result.

  1. Copy the creation prompt.
    It points your AI to the MINDPRINT creation skill on GitHub ›.
  2. Send it to your AI.
    Use an agent that knows your work, or let it interview you. Share only the context you choose.
  3. Review your MINDPRINT together.
    Correct the patterns, remove private details, and ask for a finished MINDPRINT.md.
  4. Bring it back to build mindOS.
    Import or paste your reviewed file using “Bring my own.”
